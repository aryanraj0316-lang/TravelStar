Yatrenzo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

Yatrenzo is a travel planning, booking and social application operated by Rakesh personally. Its current Android publisher is Ankit Kumar Gupta, using the developer name Eternity31. This policy covers information handled through Yatrenzo and its supporting services.

For privacy questions, complaints, access, correction or deletion requests, contact Yatrenzo Support at 1bharatgo@gmail.com. This is also the contact for questions about information handled by the operator and publisher.

2. Account, profile and activity information

Account registration collects your name, email address, mobile number and password. Passwords are stored as hashes. Optional profile information includes photographs, biography, age, gender, city or state, languages, interests and travel preferences. Guides can supply professional details, licence numbers, rates, availability and service descriptions.

We process trips, destinations, itineraries, seat requests, attendance, guide bookings, saved destinations, likes, follows, reviews, expenses, posts, stories, videos and comments that you create or use. We also store enquiries, chat messages, attachments, delivery/read receipts and online-presence information. Story views and likes are associated with accounts; a story author can see viewer and liker information. These interactions are not anonymous analytics.

3. Traveller and organizer details

Trip booking forms collect each traveller's full name, age, gender, government ID type and number. The lead traveller also supplies a phone number and an emergency contact's name and phone number. ID options currently include Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, Voter ID and PAN. These details are stored with the booking and provided to the relevant organizer, including in booking-related notifications, for trip arrangements. They are not intended to be public profile information.

Organizers who declare that they will drive can provide a driving licence number and vehicle registration number in their trip agreement. Organizer agreement details are shown with the trip and in its group chat.

Only provide another person's information when authorized to do so. For children travelling with an adult, the adult must have the necessary authority and parent or guardian permission. Do not put ID numbers or financial details in public posts, profile text or chat messages.

4. Payments and organizer payouts

For paid travel services we process booking amounts, payment/order references, payment status, refund and wallet records. Razorpay handles payment checkout where enabled. Yatrenzo does not ask you to submit full payment-card numbers or card security codes to its own booking API. Information entered into Razorpay checkout is handled by that provider.

If organizer payout onboarding is enabled, organizers linking payout accounts provide their legal name, PAN, email, phone number, beneficiary name, bank account number and IFSC. Our server sends this information to Razorpay for payout onboarding. The organizer payout record in our database stores a masked PAN and the last four bank-account digits, alongside contact, beneficiary, IFSC and provider-reference/status information. Traveller ID numbers collected for bookings are a separate data category and are not covered by this masking statement.

5. Location, maps and SOS

With device permission, location is used when you request map positioning, nearby information, routes, guide live status, location messages or SOS assistance. The current app uses foreground location requests; it does not implement continuous background location tracking. An SOS may use a device's last-known location if a fresh fix is unavailable, and the alert can identify that position as potentially stale.

When location sharing is enabled, map endpoints can make your name and recent coordinates available to other signed-in Yatrenzo users, not just people in your trip. Guide live-status locations are visible to other signed-in users only while location sharing and guide broadcasting are enabled and the position was updated within the last minute. Stop Live removes the stored guide position and prevents further updates until you start broadcasting again. Turning off location sharing removes ordinary live positions and stops guide broadcasts; turning it back on does not restart a guide broadcast.

Trip location updates are shared with fellow trip members. SOS information, including your name, location and alert message, is sent to the relevant trip participants, organizers, assigned guides, administrators and emergency contacts who can be matched to Yatrenzo accounts. Enabling the nearby SOS audience can add nearby participating users. The app does not automatically contact emergency services or guarantee that an emergency contact receives an SMS or telephone call.

You can manage device location permission and in-app sharing controls. Revoking permission stops future device location access; it does not erase coordinates already saved in chat messages or SOS alerts.

6. Photos, camera, notifications and local storage

The app requests photo/media access when you select images or videos and camera permission when you choose to take a profile photograph. Selected media is uploaded so it can be used in profiles, trips, stories or messages. The app does not upload your entire gallery.

Notification permission and push tokens support trip, chat and safety updates. Notification content can appear on your device's lock screen according to your settings. You can manage notification categories in the app and permissions in device settings.

The current Android configuration includes a microphone permission, but the current app does not implement microphone recording, voice-note recording or in-app audio/video calling. A listed permission is not a claim that recordings are collected.

Authentication tokens are stored using the native secure credential store. Preferences, profile information, cached responses and pending actions can also be stored locally for performance or offline use. Device backup and browser-storage behavior depend on the platform.

7. Purposes and technical information

We use information to operate accounts; discover and manage travel; coordinate bookings, groups and guides; deliver social content, chat and notifications; process payments and refunds; support location and SOS features; provide customer support; maintain security; investigate abuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

We also process account identifiers, consent decisions and policy versions, session timestamps, IP addresses, user-agent information, push tokens, request identifiers and diagnostic/error information. Hosting and delivery providers receive technical connection information when serving requests. Optional error reporting may include account identifiers and diagnostic details if enabled. We do not sell personal or sensitive user data or use it for targeted advertising in the current app.

8. Information visible to other people

Profiles, public trips, guide listings, posts, stories and other social content may be visible to other users according to the feature involved. A displayed name, photograph, interaction or guide agreement can identify you. Messaging content is available to the relevant room participants; messages are processed and stored by our service and are not end-to-end encrypted. Chat photographs are delivered through authenticated requests that check current chat-room membership. They are stored by our backend separately from public profile, trip and story media. Publicly shared media can be served through publicly reachable file URLs. Participants can still save or forward photographs they receive; access controls cannot recall recipient copies.

Relevant organizers receive traveller, booking and payment-status details. Trip members may receive allocation and travel coordination information. Location and SOS audiences are described above. Information copied, downloaded or forwarded by recipients may persist outside Yatrenzo.

Authorized personnel may access information for operation, support, moderation, security and legal requests. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, or for investigating abuse, protecting people and resolving disputes.

9. Service providers and international processing

Yatrenzo currently hosts its backend on Render. Database, media-storage and infrastructure providers process data on behalf of the service. Hosting providers can change as the service develops; a hosting migration does not authorize a new use of personal information.

Feature providers include Razorpay for payments/payouts; Expo and the device platform's notification services for push notifications and Expo for app updates; Brevo for transactional email where enabled; and OpenStreetMap-based map, geocoding and routing services. Google Directions may be used when configured. OpenWeatherMap receives requested weather coordinates where enabled. Map services can receive route endpoints, location/search queries, requested map areas and connection information. Externally hosted libraries and images can also receive connection information when loaded.

The policy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives technical information about visits under GitHub's own privacy practices. Our privacy page does not embed tracking scripts or a contact form. Clicking an email link opens your email application.

Providers may process information outside India. Applicable protections, retention and independent legal obligations differ by provider and processing location. Contact Yatrenzo Support with questions about providers or cross-border processing.

10. Retention and security

Account, profile, booking, communication and social information remains in service databases while needed to provide the associated features and until deletion is completed. Some records remain until a deletion request is completed; automatic expiry applies only to the data categories described below.

Live guide/map database positions are overwritten when updated and removed when ordinary location sharing is disabled. Guide positions are also removed by Stop Live and hidden if older than one minute. A scheduled job targets positions not updated for more than 24 hours, running approximately hourly while the server is running. Live trip-presence entries use a five-minute expiry in the shared presence store, or are pruned on access in its in-memory fallback. These mechanisms do not remove coordinates saved separately in chat, notifications, SOS or booking records.

Stories and story interactions do not have a general automatic expiry. Account deletion removes account-owned stories, interactions and private chat attachments from active databases. Removal of public uploaded files is queued for retry, including during storage-provider outages. A further cleanup sweep runs after previously issued upload links expire. Public-media caches, backups and recipient copies can require separate removal or expiry. Backup rotation, server logs and independent payment-provider records have separate retention; contact support for the applicable arrangements and removal requests.

We use authenticated access, password hashing and HTTPS connections to production services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

11. Access, correction and account deletion

You can edit supported profile fields and manage device permissions and in-app settings. Profile > Account & Security includes Download my data and Delete my account. The current export covers core profile, trip, messaging and safety records, but does not include every data category; email support for additional access requests.

To request deletion without using the app, email 1bharatgo@gmail.com with the subject Yatrenzo account deletion request. Include only your registered email address or mobile number so support can identify the account. We may need to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full bank/card details or copies of identity documents. This address also accepts requests to delete specific data without deleting the entire account.

The in-app deletion flow asks for your password, attempts to close bookings and refunds, revokes sessions and removes the account and associated active database records. This includes account-owned stories, story interactions, private chat attachments and identifiable system-generated booking/enquiry snapshots linked to your account. An organizer with a trip under way or unresolved refunds may need support assistance before deletion completes.

Uploaded public-media files are placed in a durable cleanup queue that retries removal. The in-app success message confirms account/database deletion and that file cleanup was queued; it does not confirm immediate erasure from every storage cache or backup. Contact support about historical uploads that cannot be reliably attributed to an account, recipient copies or specific retained information.

Some records may need to be retained for a specific legal obligation or unresolved dispute; support will explain any applicable exception when handling your request. Payment providers, recipient copies and provider backups may require separate handling. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

12. Children and accompanying travellers

Yatrenzo accounts are intended for people aged 18 or older. An adult may provide booking details for accompanying travellers, including children, only with the necessary authority and guardian permission. The current signup form does not independently verify every user's age. Contact 1bharatgo@gmail.com if you believe a child has opened an account or information has been supplied without authorization, so we can investigate and arrange removal.
